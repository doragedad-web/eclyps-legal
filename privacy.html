Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

ECLYPS may collect and store your Discord user ID, Roblox user ID, Roblox username, verification status, and verification date.

2. How We Use Your Information

This information is used to link Discord and Roblox accounts, assign server roles, prevent duplicate linking, and maintain verification records.

3. Roblox Authorization

ECLYPS uses Roblox OAuth to verify your Roblox identity. We never ask for your Roblox password.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your information. Authorized Discord server administrators may access linked account information for verification and moderation purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored verification information and restrict unauthorized access.

6. Data Removal

You may request removal of your linked account information by contacting ECLYPS administration.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as ECLYPS develops.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data removal requests, contact doragedad@gmail.com.

ECLYPS is an independent project and is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation.
